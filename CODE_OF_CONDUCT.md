# Code of Conduct

We expect contributors and participants to:

- Be respectful in code, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Focus on the technical problem, not on people.
- Accept that maintainers may close issues or PRs without lengthy explanation.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or coordinated abuse.

Enforcement: maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block users at their discretion. Report incidents to hello@icecompany.tech.
